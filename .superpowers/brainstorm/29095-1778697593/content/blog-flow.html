The Art of Slowing Down

Reflections Life

There's a particular kind of exhaustion that comes not from doing too much, but from never feeling like you've done enough. I lived in that space for years — always running, always planning the next thing before the current thing was finished.

Last month, I spent a weekend doing absolutely nothing. No plans, no agenda, no guilt. Just me, a book, a window, and the sound of rain. It was the most productive weekend I've had in years.

A quiet morning by the window — where the best ideas find you

The Myth of Productivity

We've been sold this idea that every minute needs to be optimised, that rest is earned and not given. But the older I get, the more I realise that the best things in life happen in the margins — in the unplanned hours, the aimless walks, the conversations that go nowhere and everywhere at once.

"Almost everything will work again if you unplug it for a few minutes, including you."

I'm not suggesting we abandon ambition. I'm suggesting we stop confusing motion with progress. Sometimes sitting still is the bravest thing you can do.

Another image here
The old bookshop around the corner — my favourite place to get lost

What I've Learned

The world doesn't fall apart when you take a break. Your inbox will wait. The tasks will still be there. But the sunset won't — it's happening right now, and you're either watching it or you're not.

I'm learning to choose the sunset more often.

How Blog Posts Are Stored

File Structure

posts/
├── template.html ← copy this to create a new post
├── the-art-of-slowing-down.html
├── weekend-in-mountains.html
├── books-that-changed-me.html
├── the-street-food-trail.html
└── new-year-same-me.html

blog.html ← listing page (add one entry per post)

Each post is a standalone HTML file.
Posts can be as long as you want — unlimited text, images, quotes.
Images go in images/posts/your-post-name/

To Add a New Blog Post

  1. Copy posts/template.html → posts/your-post-name.html
  2. Edit the title, date, tags, and write your content (as long as you want)
  3. Add images to images/posts/your-post-name/
  4. Open blog.html and copy a post card entry, update the title/excerpt/link

Post Template Includes

  1. Full nav bar (same as all pages)
  2. "Back to Blog" link
  3. Title, date, and tags header
  4. Content area — supports paragraphs, headings, images, blockquotes, lists
  5. Previous / Next post navigation
  6. Footer
