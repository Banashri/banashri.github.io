Why English?

A language of hopes, dreams and fulfillment. I started learning it seriously to pursue higher studies — first switching to Maths in English, then thinking in English. Sometimes it still feels foreign to me. I would never choose it to speak with my beloved. But for consuming great literature, it is the language of my choice. It is the language of my profession and my thoughts. I love this language, and I am yet to explore the full beauty of it.
