About Me

I have walked through cities where no one knew my name and made them home anyway. I have started over more times than I can count — new languages, new countries, new versions of myself. Not because it was easy, but because standing still was never an option.

I read to understand. I write to remember. I learn languages the way some people climb mountains — not to conquer them, but to prove the climb won't break me.

This space exists because some things deserve more than a passing thought. The books that kept me company on difficult nights. The words I collected in foreign tongues. The quiet stubbornness it takes to keep going when the world is loud and you are alone in it.
